Provable raids

Your base is sealed in the Ledger before anyone attacks. The battle seed is locked on Monad before tick 0. The replay's hash is recorded after. Anyone can re-run it.

A council of officers

Five AI officers — your General, the Architect, the Strategist, the Night Captain and Whisper — plan raids by simulating them, redesign your walls, and keep watch while you sleep.

Orders, not trust

The Night Captain can only act inside the orders you sealed. Every autopilot action carries their hash, and the contract refuses anything else.

Auto Mode

Hand your realm to the officers for 30 minutes to 8 hours. They collect, build, re-save your base and raid — only inside the orders you locked on Monad — until you tap Take Control.

How a raid is proven

  1. Seal. RealmLedger.sealBase(baseHash) — the defender's layout is committed.
  2. Lock the seed. Commit (server secret + your nonce), then reveal in a later block. Neither side picks the seed alone.
  3. Fight. A deterministic integer engine runs the battle on your phone at 20 Hz.
  4. Verify. The server re-simulates the replay bytes; only a match is recorded with recordResult(replayHash, …).
  5. Reputation. Your General is an ERC-8004 agent; verified battles become on-chain feedback.
